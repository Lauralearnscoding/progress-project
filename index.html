About Me

Hi, I’m Laura, a Colombian living in Canada. I was freshly diagnosed with ADHD this year. Suddenly, my entire life (and my trail of 10+ abandoned hobbies and careers) made absolute sense. My current hyperfocus? Learning how to code. It all started during my "nutrition coach" era. Deciding what to eat every single day was giving me major anxiety. Between my neurodivergent brain paralysis and a very uncooperative gut (shoutout to IBS), even picking a takeout order felt like a boss battle. Existing meal planning apps didn't quite hit, so I decided I needed my own app in Spanish, tailored specifically to my chaos. Plot twist: I had zero coding skills. I managed to glue together a surprisingly functional web app using Bubble and took a quick beginner class to understand what was going on under the hood. To my surprise, I fell in love with the building process. The hyperfocus hit hard, and I realized I didn't just want to rely on no-code tools forever. I wanted to actually learn how to build stuff from scratch. So here we are. This page is my live, unfiltered progress tracker for learning HTML and CSS. If nothing else, it’s a place for future me to look back, flex a little, and be proud of not giving up after two weeks.
